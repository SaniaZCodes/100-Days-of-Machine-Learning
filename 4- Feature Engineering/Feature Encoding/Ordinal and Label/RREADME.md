# Ordinal Encoding & Label Encoding 🔤

## What is Encoding?

Encoding converts categorical data into numerical data.

Example:

Male → 0

Female → 1

---

## Ordinal Encoding

Used when categories have a natural order.

Examples:

- Small, Medium, Large
- Low, Medium, High

Encoding:

Small → 0

Medium → 1

Large → 2

---

## Label Encoding

Assigns numbers to categories.

Examples:

- Red, Blue, Green
- Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad

Encoding:

Red → 0

Blue → 1

Green → 2

---

## Key Difference

Ordinal Encoding:
✅ Order Exists

Label Encoding:
✅ Categories get labels
❌ Order may not exist

---

## Golden Line 🌟

Convert categories into numbers before giving data to ML models.
