# PCA Part 2 - Problem Formulation & Step-by-Step Solution 📊

## Goal

Reduce dimensions while preserving maximum information.

---

## PCA Steps

### Step 1

Standardize the data.

---

### Step 2

Find the direction with maximum variance.

---

### Step 3

Create the First Principal Component (PC1).

PC1 contains maximum variance.

---

### Step 4

Create the Second Principal Component (PC2).

PC2 contains less variance.

---

### Step 5

Keep important components and remove less important ones.

---

## Important Terms

### Eigenvector

Represents direction.

---

### Eigenvalue

Represents variance.

---

## Explained Variance Ratio

Shows how much information each principal component contains.

Example:

PC1 → 95%

PC2 → 5%

---

## Key Idea

Higher Variance
↓
More Information

---

## Golden Line 🌟

PCA creates principal components and keeps those with maximum variance while removing less informative dimensions.
